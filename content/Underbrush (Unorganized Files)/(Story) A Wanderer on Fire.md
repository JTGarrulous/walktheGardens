 In the Garden of [[Klysovinna Third]], there is a tale that often sparks around communal traveling fires. 
 The roving dairy monks of Klysovinna Third speak of a young woman that appears mysteriously around any open flame that is uncovered to the night sky. 
 She is often depicted with pale red skin, scales of shimmering gold upon her neck and cheeks, and eyes of darkened coal. In some tales, she wears a flowing, layered gown of soft velvet and a circlet of bone dry branches upon gold and ash hair. In others, she wears nothing at all, save for soot covering her entirely.

The Wanderer on Fire is mostly spoken of as a joyous, if not peaceful encounter; she comes to the flame asking for the attention of those around it. She dances through the night with a wild abandon, often asking others to dance along with her.
Many personal accounts of the Wanderer on Fire speak on how she not only lifts the communal flames higher, but raises the souls of those who dance with her; there is a sense of togetherness and aliveness that she brings to the road-worn and spirit-weary.

In other cautionary tales, there are whispers of the same Wanderer on Fire stalking the sleeping [[Taltree|taltrees]], and coming no closer when those around the communal fire are unwelcome.  She gets no closer than the edges of the dark, and disappears before any can find her.

In the case of a joyous night, the stories tell of her only request of payment: an ember from the communal flame, so that when she leaves, the fire burns just a little dimmer without her presence, and she is welcomed once more to stoke the flame.

But when she disappears in those nights where none around the fire are welcome... she takes all heat and bright with her, leaving only darkness behind.
