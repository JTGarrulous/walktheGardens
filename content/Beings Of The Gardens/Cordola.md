The Cordola is a type of sedentary, tubular creature that is completely stationary, with a thick outer blue skin and a broccoli-esque clump atop its head. It is sightless, and many Folk find that they sway willingly with the breeze.

- Loves to be eaten. Most Beings can't say this, but the Cordola can!
- Exist to be eaten by others; fat, boneless, endearingly lazy. 
	- They feel no pain, and have an innate sense of cyclical living; they breed quite literally for the joy that they bring to others.
- When someone burps after consumption, tiny Cordola eggs float out in bubbles, and  are grown through a spore-like mycelium system.
- Often found in huge, sprawling clumps.

A word of warning for those looking to harvest the Cordola: do not take more than you need; the peaceful Cordola are known to knock down harvesters and suffocate them beneath their masses. 

Know this: the Cordola realize when they are being harvested for greed instead of need.

Companion creature to the [[Bonyin]].
