A type of bee that makes its hive within books.

Often found in old libraries, ancient archives, and abandoned bookstores, an inkbee hive will range from a single tome, to whole shelves, to entire bookcases. Their buzzing resonates high and low, creating a type of echolocation that they have developed from living in dark places, where the Light cannot guide them like other bee variants.

The buzzing can sometimes resemble the sound of words that are contained inside of the books they convert into hive. There are accounts of Shadewhisperers and Chroniclers living amongst the inkbees and listening to their song, rewriting the lost books day by day.

The honey of inkbees is often a dark, rich black, and smells of old books. It is not poisonous, but consuming too much causes mild misophonia, auditory hallucinations while reading, and temporary dyslexia.
