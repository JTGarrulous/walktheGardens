[[- The Grand Index -]]

Well, hello there, You!

If You've discovered this place, that means that You've found a story of mine floating through the big ol' river we all inhabit. 

My name is Garrulous, and I thank You deeply for being here. 
I am the Narrator of the place known as the Gardens: A fantastical polyverse a few hundred trillion years in the future from when You're reading this.

This is a digital garden, which serves as a grounds where I record and transcribe various aspects of the Gardens. Here, you will find scraps of information at various stages of completion; the Gardens, after all, are vast and numinously numerous--no matter how hard I try, I fear that I will never truly know them all.

But that does not mean that we do not try. Right?

For now, I would recommend you start at the [[Trailhead -- Source]]. 

See you around, Traveler.
