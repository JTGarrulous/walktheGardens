Also known as lapsap, and lapis sapuli, in humorous contexts 

A naturally occurring substance found in trees and other plants that produce sap.

In the Garden of [[(Garden) Ereketit]], lapis lazuli is a naturally occurring substance found in water, is consumed by the trees, and produces lapisap. The amount of lapis found in water is minimal there, and must be condensed by the trees into something usable.

In many other Gardens, trees found often on lapis lazuli or opal deposits will produce their respective sap.
