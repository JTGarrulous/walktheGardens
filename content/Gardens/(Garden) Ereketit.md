# Garden Template 

Small population, less than 5,000 Folkish inhabitants. Garden is circular and flat with no underside, humid conditions, lush growth and tall trees with sparse, yet large, sturdy branches.
The [[Beings]] known as [[Diffinera]] roam the jungle laden landscape, hunting for their main prey, which is anything that carries Memory within them.

While all things carry Memory, there are dense and soaked sources that the Diffinera are more inclined to: namely the Folk of Ereketit. In lieu of capturing these Folk, they can consume smaller, less fruitful Memories from other Beings, and [[Shades]] as well. 

The Folk live in roaming, treebound villages that make a slow, neverending exodus from one lost place of safety to another. It is something ingrained in their minds, that running from the Diffinera is the most natural workings of their world. In their language, Diffinera means 'the wind', because those mnemophage Beings are always pushing them forward.

Because of this interaction, the Folk of Ereketit have formed their cultural practice around the act of exporting their Memories, so that the Diffinera have less to feed on, in hopes that their chase will falter one day. While the practice of the [[Loom]] does not exist here, the Folk of Ereketit have devised a unique way of using their blood (See: [[Water (Breath)]] as a conduit to pull Memories from their Threadline, and place it into the art that they create.

These Folk have created what they call Ranknakuit (Rank-Nah-Kweet), which is a mixture of blood, [[(Material) Lapisap and Opalsap]], and other trace materials to create a personally identifiable material that is used in nearly all forms of artistic expression in Ereketit.
