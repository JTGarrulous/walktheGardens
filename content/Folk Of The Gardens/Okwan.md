# The okwanFolk

Okwan are some of the first folk of the Gardens, characterized by muscular, serpentine bodies, mixed with canine aesthetics and qualities. 
These Folk are far-flung descendants of the serpentine legends that surround the dragons of [[Root]]; they can trace their heritage to crafted [[Riverian]] species such as dragonkin//dragonborn, or the [[Wyrkosta]]. 
The okwan are embodied, crystallized passion, finding vivid emotions coming easily to them.
# Okwan Husk Physiology

Okwans are scaled creatures, whose skin texture and color vastly vary. Some okwan have smooth, small scales, while others have long and feather-like scales that overlap. 
Coloring is generally a collection of three separate colors:
- Brighter scales on the neck, shoulders, arms, head, palms , outer thighs, tependah, and bottoms of feet.
- Paler scales are on the chest, stomach, groin, inner thighs, and tops of hands and feet. 
- After reaching maturity, many okwan gain a shade variance to their scales. For example, a crimson scaled okwan might find a more vivid edging to that crimson across most of their body.
	- Some chimeric reactions with okwan scales caused them to form a type of vitiligo, where their scales might take on a color from a past life. 

The bone structure of an okwan takes on the humanoid shape of most Folk, while mixed with not only features of a serpent, but the strong jaw and ears of a canine. Some Folk report meeting quadrupedal okwan, but these are not common.

Some accessory features of an okwan might include a crest, fins, or small bony protrusions not unlike the brynn of the [[Keesat]]. Few variants of okwan are able to secrete poison, while others might be able to breathe flames, or in one edge case, a caffeine-based mist. 

The okwan have a limb called a tependah: a long, thick tail with a four-fingered hand at the tip. The tependah is a primary feature of the okwan, lending to highly increased dexterity and agility. 

Most okwanFolk, in addition, are able to breathe under water. Some variants, depending on the Garden in which they are grown in, are able to breathe in other liquids, such as oil or mayonnaise. Oftentimes, these Husks are ill-suited for traveling to other Gardens due to the vast biological differences that might hamper their journeys. 

# Okwan Subtypes
## Okwan-Asa

The most prominent type of Okwan Husk, as described above. While most Okwan-asa are land dwelling creatures, some still choose to find their lives beneath the waves, though at a small disadvantage to their Okwan-suvee kin. 
## Okwan-Suvee

A type of water-bound Okwan whose tependah has become their entire lower half. Without legs, the Okwan-suvee are usually not fans of the land, despite still having the ability to breathe outside of the water. The Okwan-suvee are generally found in Gardens fully submerged in water, lava, or any other liquid that a Folk might learn to swim in.
# Okwan Demeanor

> [!Note]
This section attempts to ascribe a general demeanor one might feel when inhabiting this type of Husk; this is not descriptive of every Folk that is this particular Husk type, nor does it attempt to justify the actions a [[soulknot]] might take in this Husk. 
All Husks are cursed with a [[Hunger]] at their creation, but this Hunger does not always become fully enmeshed with the soulknot planted within. 

The Okwan Husk is one of passion and high, [[Fire]]-bound emotions, such as lust, anger, rage, and grief. The [[Hunger of Power]] often guides an Okwan to either make emotion-driven decisions.

In Gardens where the concept of 'might makes right' prevails, okwanFolk are often found in positions of power, being naturally inclined to climb a hill of bodies to stand atop them. Many okwan in Gardens where violence is looked down upon or prohibited generally struggle to thrive without proper guidance. As with all Hungers, a mature okwan that can control themselves will find a vivid and pleasurable existence living in harmony with their sometimes too-strong emotions.
